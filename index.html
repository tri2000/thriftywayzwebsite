ThriftyWayz is the sourcing app for resellers: every thrift store and yard sale near you, plus a profit check you can run right in the aisle.

THRIFT TAB

Every thrift store near you, sorted by what's open now.

The closest open store comes first, with today's deals like 50% off red tags. See what's closing soon and what opens later, then tap for directions or add a stop to your route.

  • Closest open store first
  • Closing-soon times
  • Today's deals and tag sales
MAP TAB

Every store in the area, on one map.

See all the thrift stores, outlets and antique malls around you, with your saved spots marked. Search, filter, or flip to a list.

  • Your spots plus everything nearby
  • Search and filter
  • Map or list view
PROFIT TAB

Should I buy it? Know before you check out.

Enter what you'd pay and what it sells for. See your profit on 7 platforms after fees, your return, and the most you should pay to hit your goal.

  • Vinted, Poshmark, Depop, TikTok Shop, eBay Live, Whatnot, eBay
  • Max buy price for your profit goal
  • Return and shipping cost at a glance
SALES TAB

Every yard sale near you, happening now and tomorrow.

See which yard, garage and rummage sales are open right now, when they end, and what they're selling. Hosting one? Post your sale in a few taps.

  • Happening now, with end times
  • Tags like Toys, Furniture and Antiques
  • Post your own sale
